# 1. The PS — in simple words

### PS 26238
**Unified Scholarship Mobile Application for Tribal Students**

The fundamental problem is that scholarship-related services for tribal students are spread across multiple systems.

The intended solution is **not simply another scholarship website**.

It is essentially:

> **A unified layer that brings scholarship discovery, application, document reuse, verification, status tracking, payment information, assistance, and beneficiary-gap identification together for tribal students.**

The research we conducted found that the important gap is specifically the **verified unification of the MoTA/ST scholarship ecosystem**, rather than merely creating another scholarship-search website. Pasted markdown(20260928-095245)

# 8. The most important verification design decision

What happens when verification fails?

Do **not** do:

```text
Verification failed
       ↓
REJECT
```

And don't do:

```text
API unavailable
       ↓
PASS
```

Instead:

```text
Verification
     │
     ├── PASS ──────> Continue
     │
     ├── MISMATCH ──> Human Review
     │
     └── SOURCE DOWN > Verification Pending
```

For example:

> Income certificate couldn't be verified.

The system should tell the student:

**Why:** source mismatch  
**Status:** Manual review required  
**Action:** No automatic rejection  
**Reviewer:** Institution/Nodal officer

This is one of the strongest architectural points in the research. Pasted markdown(20260928-095245)

> What happens when verification fails?

Answer:
