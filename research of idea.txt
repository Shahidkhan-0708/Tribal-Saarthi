# 2. The real student problem isn't simply "finding scholarships"

This is important.

Your solution shouldn't be presented as:

> "We will make a website where students can search scholarships."

That's too basic.

The actual problem is closer to:

```text
Student
  ↓
Finds scheme
  ↓
Checks eligibility
  ↓
Registers
  ↓
Uploads documents
  ↓
Verification
  ↓
Deficiency/mismatch?
  ↓
Fix/resubmit
  ↓
Institute verification
  ↓
Department verification
  ↓
Sanction
  ↓
DBT
  ↓
Payment
```

There are many stages.

The current NSP itself shows separate application, defective-application verification, institute verification and departmental verification stages. [Scholarships.gov.in](https://scholarships.gov.in/All-Scholarships?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

So **application submission is only one part of the problem**.
