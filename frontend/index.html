Structure preserved

Two-rail desktop shells, multi-column officer tables, three-step wizard and mobile bottom bar keep the exact widget architecture of the wireframes.

Hairline discipline

No shadows, no gradients: 1px rules divide every region, radii stop at 8px, and status colour lives in soft pastel tags rather than fills.

Honest sample data

All records are the labelled sample case from the wireframes. No invented metrics, no fabricated official claims, no imagery substitutes.
